Receipts Express

Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy

Washington My Health My Data Act · Effective 8 August 2026

← Back to Receipts Express

Receipts Express runs entirely in your web browser. It has no server, no accounts, and no analytics. Everything you scan or enter is processed and stored only on your own device and is never transmitted to the author or to anyone else.

This policy is published by Jordan Campbell ("the author"), an individual resident in the State of Washington, United States; the project's GitHub repository, linked below, is the contact channel for it.

Categories of consumer health data collected, and the purpose of collection

None.

The author does not buy, rent, access, retain, receive, acquire, infer, derive, or otherwise process any consumer health data. The app performs no health inference and derives no health status: it stores an image of a receipt and the merchant, date, and amount you confirm. If you scan a receipt from a pharmacy or a medical provider, it stays on your device exactly like every other receipt.

Categories of sources of consumer health data

None.

There is no source to describe, because the author receives no data of any kind from the app: it has no server to receive it and makes no request that carries it.

Categories of consumer health data shared

None.

Nothing is shared because nothing is held: the author receives no data of any kind from the app, so there is no category of it to pass on.

Third parties and affiliates with whom consumer health data is shared

None.

There are no affiliates, no service providers, no processors, and no contractors. The author has no access to your data and therefore nothing to share.

Sale of consumer health data

The author does not sell consumer health data, has never sold it, and does not offer it for sale.

Geofencing

The author does not operate any geofence.

The app never requests your location, and no geofence is or has been placed around any facility providing in-person health care services.

How to exercise your rights

The Act gives you the right to confirm whether your consumer health data is being collected, to access it, to withdraw consent, and to have it deleted. Because the author holds no data about you, there is nothing for the author to confirm, access, or delete. You hold the only copy, and you can act on it directly and immediately:

  • Delete an individual expense, or delete a report — which also deletes its expenses and their receipt images.
  • Erase everything at once using your browser's "clear site data" or website-data controls for this site.

To submit a request or an appeal anyway, or to tell the author that something on this page is inaccurate, open an issue at github.com/Jwtc2000/Receipts-Express/issues. The author will respond as promptly as reasonably possible, and in any event will aim to respond within 45 days. If a request is denied, you may contact the Washington State Attorney General at atg.wa.gov/file-complaint.

This notice is published for transparency. It is not an admission that the author is a "regulated entity" or a "small business" as those terms are defined in RCW 19.373.010.
